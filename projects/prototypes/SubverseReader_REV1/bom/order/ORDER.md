3. **Newark:** paste `SR-n.newark.txt` into Newark's Quick Paste / BOM upload.
4. **Direct:** the remaining vendors (Good Display panels, Seeed, Adafruit, AliExpress cells, LCSC passives, JLCPCB boards) are listed per variant below.
