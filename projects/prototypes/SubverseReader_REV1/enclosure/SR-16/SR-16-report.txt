Assembly:
- Glass drops into the front shell face-down, taped to the bezel border with 0.2 mm double-sided tape (it is not clamped from behind).
- Driver board + XIAO at the bottom behind the glass, USB-C out of the bottom edge; the panel FPC folds back onto the driver board's connector.
- Keys: three B3F-1000 on an 8 x 40 mm perfboard strip glued to the rail, plungers against the printed caps in the right wall.
- Driver stack assumed 12.5 mm (XIAO in its socket). Soldering the XIAO flat to the driver board instead brings it to about 6.5 mm: set drv_stack and rerun for a ~12 mm pass.

Print: PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 25% gyroid. Front shell face down (bezel on the bed),
back shell back down; key caps flange down. No supports, except the USB-C and lanyard
slot bridges if your printer sags over 14 mm.

MEASURE before the final print: see the MEASURE notes at the top of make_enclosure.py.
