# Enclosures: SR-15 Dial Pass, SR-16 Setlist, SR-16N (no keys), SR-16SD (card) and SR-16L (long)

Two-part printed shells (front bezel and back tray) held by four M2 screws, plus small printed
key plungers (none on SR-16N). Everything is generated by `make_enclosure.py` in Blender 5 from the numbers at the
top of the script. To change a dimension, edit it there and rerun; don't edit the STLs.

```
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b -P make_enclosure.py -- [SR-15] [SR-16] [SR-16N] [SR-16SD] [SR-16L] [--ref DIR]
```

With no variant named it builds all five. SR-16SD is the SR-16N shell with a card slot; its
numbers are SR-16N's unless noted.

Each variant folder has print-oriented STLs (millimetres), a `.blend` with the shells and
reference electronics assembled, a `-layout.svg` mechanical layout (SR-16 family: back view and side
section, every component numbered), a `-fit.png` preview (front shell inside-up, back shell,
electronics) and a `-report.txt` with sizes, volumes, the manifold check and an interference
check of every shell against the electronics.

## Printing

PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 25 % gyroid.
- **Front shell:** print face down, bezel on the bed. The window chamfer faces the bed.
- **Back shell:** print back down.
- **Key plungers and caps:** print flange down. They are tiny, so print them with a shell or
  use a brim.
- **Supports:** none for SR-15: its only bridges are the USB-C opening and the lanyard slot
  (14 mm). The SR-16 and SR-16N back shells are stepped and need supports touching the bed
  under the shelf.

## SR-15 Dial Pass (Elecrow CrowPanel 4.2in)

Turned portrait, the board's controls land on the **bottom edge** in a row: EXIT, dial, MENU. Your
thumbs are there when you lift the pass to read. The other edges:
- **Top edge:** USB-C, left of the lanyard ear.
- **Left edge:** microSD.
- **Back:** BOOT and RESET, through two pinholes (upper right).

Positions come from Elecrow's Eagle file and STEP model (repo
`Elecrow-RD/CrowPanel-ESP32-4.2-E-paper-HMI-Display-with-400-300`).

- **Board:** drops into the front shell face down. The PCB border bears on a step in the bezel,
  and the glass sits 0.1 mm under the bezel lip (add 0.3 mm foam on the glass border if it
  rattles). Four pads on the back shell press on the PCB back near the corners.
- **Board holes:** the board isn't screwed down. Elecrow's STEP shows six M3 holes, but the Eagle
  file drills none and Elecrow has shipped three PCBA revisions. If yours has the holes, a later
  shell can use them and lose ~5 mm of width.
- **Dial:** pokes out of a slot in the bottom wall, with a thumb scallop around it.
- **MENU and EXIT:** pressed through printed plungers captured in a pocket in the wall. The
  plunger flange rests against the side switch's actuator.
- **Battery connector:** the SH1.0 socket faces the top edge. The plug and its lead bend into a
  pocket inside the lanyard ear.
- **Battery:** a 603450 cell (50 × 34 × 6) lies on the back plate between ribs, below the
  middle, where only 0603 parts sit on the board. Its lead runs through the slide switch in the
  left-wall cradle (lower left) to the SH1.0 socket.
- **Screws:** M2×6 thread-forming screws go into Ø1.6 pilots in the front-shell corner bosses.
  The heads sit in Ø3.9 pockets in the back.

**Fit check against Elecrow's STEP:** convert the STEP with `step_to_stl.py` (instructions in
its header) and run with `--ref DIR`. On 2026-11-16 this gave 0.00 mm³ of interference for both
shells against the board, the cell and the switch.

**Check on the board you receive:**
- Which PCBA version it is (V1.0, V1.2 or V1.2A). Compare the dial, side keys, USB-C and
  microSD positions with the STEP model.
- **Glass active-area offset.** The window assumes the panel's FPC edge, on the left in
  portrait, has a 10.1 mm border and the others about 3.2 mm (`SR15["aa"]`). Power the panel
  with a full-black page and check the window edges.
- **JS102011SAQN slide switch:** the cradle assumes a body of about 8.6 × 3.5 × 3.6 mm.

## SR-16 Setlist and SR-16N (Elecrow 3.7in panel, Seeed driver board, XIAO ESP32-C5)

Rev D (2026-11-25). The first shell folded the panel's own ribbon onto the driver board and
assumed a 12.5 mm stack. On the bench the ribbon slipped out twice, the stack measured 27 mm,
and the USB-C did not reach an edge. Rev C moved the stack to a pod at the bottom and added a
ribbon extension. Rev D takes the driver board from Seeed's STEP model and replaces the snug
USB-C hole with a cable channel. The reasoning is in
[the design doc](../docs/DESIGN-SubverseReader.md) under "SR-16 layout, rev D".

What goes where, seen from the front:
- **Glass:** portrait, with its ribbon tail at the **top**. Set `2 epd-rot!` on the pass so the
  picture is the right way up.
- **Tail:** folds once over the top edge of the glass, in its marked bending area (0.5 to 2.5 mm
  past the edge). Its last 7 mm lie on the back, gold fingers facing the back.
- **Coupler:** a 24-pin 0.5 mm FFC coupler board with top-contact connectors (Adafruit 4524 or
  the same kind), on foam tape on the back of the glass, connectors facing the back, its top
  edge 4 mm below the top of the glass, between two fences. Pads in the back shell press it down.
- **Jumper:** 24-pin 0.5 mm FFC, 60 mm, same-side contacts, fingers facing the back at both
  ends: from the coupler down over the cell, through the doorway in the step, into the driver
  board.
- **Antenna:** the XIAO's stick-on antenna on the inside of the pod's left wall.
- **Cell:** 603450 lying across the shelf (50 × 34 mm), against the glass.
- **Tray** (printed): a 1 mm plate on the back of the glass in the pod. The driver board stands
  on it on its pin tips between four pegs and four corner stops, so no pin touches the glass.
- **Driver board and XIAO:** in the pod on the glass's centreline, component side to the back,
  ribbon connector up, USB-C pointing down.
- **USB-C channel:** the port is 5 mm inside the bottom edge at the end of a channel 14 mm wide,
  cut through the bottom wall and the back, with sides and a roof inside the pod. It reaches
  4 mm further toward the glass than a plug needs, so the port does not have to line up with
  anything.
- **Battery switch:** the driver board's own, about 14 mm inside the right wall. A 10 × 5 mm
  window in that wall lets a toothpick reach it.
- **Keys (SR-16 only):** three B3F-1000 keys (PREV = D4, MENU = D7, NEXT = D9, 14 mm apart) on an
  8 × 40 mm perfboard strip glued to a rail on the shelf, behind printed caps in the right wall.
- **BOOT pinhole (SR-16N):** Ø1.8 mm through the back of the pod, placed from Seeed's KiCad
  board for the XIAO ESP32-C5.
- **Lanyard:** a slot through a solid boss high in the top band, clear of the fold.

The back is stepped: the pod is the bottom 52 mm and 32.8 mm thick, the shelf above it 11.6 mm
(12.7 mm with keys). **Print the back shell back down with supports touching the bed only:** the
shelf hangs 21 mm above the bed. Tray plate down.

The pod is exactly as deep as the measured stack. Seeed's models give 14.4 mm for a XIAO on
standard short pins, which would make the pod about 20 mm: set `drv_stack` and rerun.

Measured by ruler: `drv_stack` 27 mm. From drawings and models: the glass, active area and tail
(Good Display's GDEY037T03 drawing); the driver board, its switch and connectors (Seeed's STEP
model); the BOOT button and the USB-C's 2.5 mm overhang (Seeed's KiCad board for the XIAO).
Still assumed, so check before the final print:
- **Stack height.** The ruler and the models disagree by 12 mm.
- **Coupler** 20 × 12 × 3.6 mm (`coupler`), and that the tail reaches its mouth (`tail_reach`).
- **The ribbon run is a straight extension.** Check with a meter, tail pad to driver-board pad,
  before power: this ribbon carries the panel's high voltages.
- **Battery sense:** whether the XIAO's own battery pads see a cell on the driver board's socket.

Built 2026-11-25 with box stand-ins: every part manifold, 0 mm³ interference against the
glass, driver board (with its switch, battery socket and ribbon connector), XIAO, a USB-C plug
in the port, cell and coupler.

### SR-16SD: the card slot

The SR-16N shell with a microSD breakout (Adafruit 4682, 25.4 × 22.8 × 3.5 mm) standing on edge
in the pod, left of the driver board seen from the front:
- **Breakout:** socket toward the left wall, card end against the bottom wall, stuck with 0.5 mm
  foam tape to a fin on the tray. The tray prints plate down, fin up (24 mm tall).
- **Card slot:** 12.5 × 2.6 mm in the bottom edge, 9.2 mm from the left side, with a thumbnail
  relief. The card is drawn 2.5 mm proud of the breakout's edge.
- **Antenna:** on the inside of the pod's right wall, toward the back, clear of the switch window.
- **Wires:** 3V3, GND, SCK = D8, MOSI = D10, MISO = D9, CS = D7, to the driver board's pad row.
- **Assumed:** which edge of the breakout the card enters, where along it, how far the card
  stands proud, and the socket height (`sd`). Check on the part and rerun.

Same build check: manifold, 0 mm³ interference, including the breakout and a card in the slot.

## SR-16L Setlist, long: nothing stacked

SR-16N made long instead of thick: 57.0 × 156.8 mm, 11.6 mm thick, 15.1 mm at the chin, against
SR-16N's 119.5 mm and 32.8 mm. Three printed parts (front, back, chin cap), no supports.

- **Glass:** ribbon tail at the **bottom** (rotation 0, the firmware's default).
- **Driver board:** in a chin below the glass, components toward the **front**, ribbon connector
  toward the glass. The tail goes straight in, 3.5 mm, with no fold, no coupler and no jumper.
- **Chin cap:** drops into the front shell from behind, before the board. It stands 3.5 mm proud
  of the bezel to clear the board's parts, carries three rests the board lies on, and has a slot
  over the battery switch.
- **XIAO:** a **bare** XIAO ESP32-C5 (no pin headers) on foam tape on the back of the glass,
  components to the back, USB-C through the right wall seen from the front, 65 mm above the
  bottom edge. BOOT and RESET pinholes in the back.
- **Wires:** nine, 30 AWG, about 45 mm, XIAO pad to the same-named pad in the driver board's two
  pad rows: 5V, GND, 3V3, D0, D1, D2, D3, D8, D10.
- **Cell:** 603450 across, behind the glass above the XIAO, on the driver board's socket.

Check before the final print: where the tail leaves the glass and how high the connector's slot
is above the board (`tail_z`, `fpc_slot`), since together they set the board's depth and a wrong
figure bends the tail; the USB-C hole, which is a plain hole here, not a channel; and which of
the driver board's pads are the two pad rows (read from Seeed's schematic, not from the board).

## Files


- `make_enclosure.py`: the generator. All dimensions live in the `SR15`, `SR16` and `SR16N` dicts.
- `step_to_stl.py`: converts Elecrow's STEP to a board-only STL for `--ref`. It needs
  `cadquery-ocp`. Elecrow's repo has no license, so the STEP and STL stay out of this repo.
- `SR-15/`, `SR-16/`, `SR-16N/`, `SR-16SD/` and `SR-16L/`: generated outputs, built with box stand-ins.
