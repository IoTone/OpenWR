# Enclosures: SR-15 Dial Pass and SR-16 Setlist

Two-part printed shells (front bezel and back tray) held by four M2 screws, plus small printed
key plungers. Everything is generated by `make_enclosure.py` in Blender 5 from the numbers at the
top of the script. To change a dimension, edit it there and rerun; don't edit the STLs.

```
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b -P make_enclosure.py -- [SR-15] [SR-16] [--ref DIR]
```

Each variant folder has print-oriented STLs (millimetres), a `.blend` with the shells and
reference electronics assembled, a `-fit.png` preview (front shell inside-up, back shell,
electronics) and a `-report.txt` with sizes, volumes, the manifold check and an interference
check of every shell against the electronics.

| | SR-15 Dial Pass | SR-16 Setlist |
|---|---|---|
| Outer size | 93.8 × 123.8 × 13.4 mm (incl. lanyard ear) | 64.0 × 113.5 × 16.8 mm |
| Parts | front, back, 2 key plungers | front, back, 3 key caps |
| Plastic | ~41 cm³ solid, ~40 g printed | ~25 cm³ solid, ~25 g printed |
| Screws | 4 × M2×6 pan head, thread-forming for plastic | same |
| Drawn against | Elecrow's Eagle board and STEP model | listing dimensions only |
| Confidence | high: fit-checked against the STEP model, 0 mm³ interference | first print is a fit test |

## Printing

PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 25 % gyroid.
- **Front shell:** print face down, bezel on the bed. The window chamfer faces the bed.
- **Back shell:** print back down.
- **Key plungers and caps:** print flange down. They are tiny, so print them with a shell or
  use a brim.
- **Supports:** none needed. The only bridges are the USB-C opening and the lanyard slot
  (14 mm).

## SR-15 Dial Pass (Elecrow CrowPanel 4.2in)

Turned portrait, the board's controls land on the **bottom edge** in a row: EXIT, dial, MENU. Your
thumbs are there when you lift the pass to read. The other edges:
- **Top edge:** USB-C, left of the lanyard ear.
- **Left edge:** microSD.
- **Back:** BOOT and RESET, through two pinholes (upper right).

Positions come from Elecrow's Eagle file and STEP model (repo
`Elecrow-RD/CrowPanel-ESP32-4.2-E-paper-HMI-Display-with-400-300`).

- **Board:** drops into the front shell face down. The PCB border bears on a step in the bezel,
  and the glass sits 0.1 mm under the bezel lip (add 0.3 mm foam on the glass border if it
  rattles). Four pads on the back shell press on the PCB back near the corners.
- **Board holes:** the board isn't screwed down. Elecrow's STEP shows six M3 holes, but the Eagle
  file drills none and Elecrow has shipped three PCBA revisions. If yours has the holes, a later
  shell can use them and lose ~5 mm of width.
- **Dial:** pokes out of a slot in the bottom wall, with a thumb scallop around it.
- **MENU and EXIT:** pressed through printed plungers captured in a pocket in the wall. The
  plunger flange rests against the side switch's actuator.
- **Battery connector:** the SH1.0 socket faces the top edge. The plug and its lead bend into a
  pocket inside the lanyard ear.
- **Battery:** a 603450 cell (50 × 34 × 6) lies on the back plate between ribs, below the
  middle, where only 0603 parts sit on the board. Its lead runs through the slide switch in the
  left-wall cradle (lower left) to the SH1.0 socket.
- **Screws:** M2×6 thread-forming screws go into Ø1.6 pilots in the front-shell corner bosses.
  The heads sit in Ø3.9 pockets in the back.

**Fit check against Elecrow's STEP:** convert the STEP with `step_to_stl.py` (instructions in
its header) and run with `--ref DIR`. On 2026-11-16 this gave 0.00 mm³ of interference for both
shells against the board, the cell and the switch.

**Check on the board you receive:**
- Which PCBA version it is (V1.0, V1.2 or V1.2A). Compare the dial, side keys, USB-C and
  microSD positions with the STEP model.
- **Glass active-area offset.** The window assumes the panel's FPC edge, on the left in
  portrait, has a 10.1 mm border and the others about 3.2 mm (`SR15["aa"]`). Power the panel
  with a full-black page and check the window edges.
- **JS102011SAQN slide switch:** the cradle assumes a body of about 8.6 × 3.5 × 3.6 mm.

## SR-16 Setlist (Elecrow 3.7in panel, Seeed driver board, XIAO ESP32-C5)

Glass portrait in the front shell. Behind it:
- **Driver board and XIAO:** at the bottom, with the XIAO's USB-C out of the bottom edge and
  the panel FPC folded back onto the driver board.
- **Cell:** a 603450 cell above them.
- **Keys:** three B3F-1000 keys (PREV, MENU, NEXT, 14 mm apart) on the right edge, mounted on an
  8 × 40 mm perfboard strip glued to a rail in the back shell. They press printed caps that pass
  through Ø4 holes in the right wall.
- **Slide switch:** on the left wall, level with the cell.
- **Lanyard:** the slot goes through a solid boss in the top band.

**Everything here is from listings, so measure before the final print:**
- **Panel glass:** 53 × 93 × 1 mm per the listing (`SR16["glass"]`). The listing also mentions a
  67 mm carrier board. If one arrives, the shell must grow.
- **Active-area borders:** assumed 2.98 mm on the sides, 3.0 mm at the top and 8.46 mm on the
  FPC (bottom) side (`aa_border`).
- **Driver board:** 43 × 26 mm per RobotShop (`drv`). Seeed publishes no drawing.
- **Driver stack height with the XIAO fitted** (`drv_stack`, 12.5 mm assumed). This sets the
  thickness. Soldering the XIAO flat to the driver board instead of using the socket brings the
  stack to about 6.5 mm and the pass to about 12.5 mm.
- **Which way the XIAO's USB-C faces** on the driver board, and its height above the back
  (`usb_z_from_back`). The shell assumes it points down, centred on the glass. The driver board
  also has its own battery switch and JST connector, so the separate slide switch may turn out
  to be unnecessary.

The glass is taped into the bezel with 0.2 mm double-sided tape on its border. Nothing clamps it
from behind.

## Files

- `make_enclosure.py`: the generator. All dimensions live in the `SR15` and `SR16` dicts.
- `step_to_stl.py`: converts Elecrow's STEP to a board-only STL for `--ref`. It needs
  `cadquery-ocp`. Elecrow's repo has no license, so the STEP and STL stay out of this repo.
- `SR-15/` and `SR-16/`: generated outputs, built with box stand-ins.
