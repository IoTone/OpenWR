# Hiroglass REV1: what is buildable

Generated by `sourcing.py`. Sources checked **2026-11-10**; in-hand dates assume an order placed **2026-11-10**. Edit the script, not this file.

Two buyers are compared. **US** buys only from US sellers shipping from US stock. **Japan** buys only from sellers that deliver to Japan: Japanese shops first, DigiKey Japan (ships from the US, duty paid), and sellers in China or Hong Kong where nothing else exists.

Cost is parts plus one shipping fee per seller, in US dollars (¥158.29 to the dollar), with Japan's 10% consumption tax where a shop lists prices before tax. US sales tax is not included. *In hand* is the slowest line's delivery; PCB assembly, 3D printing and bring-up time come after it.

## Rules that affect the build

- **US tariffs on boards from China:** JLCPCB collects 35% or more up front on US orders and requires duty-paid shipping, so OSH Park (US-made, free shipping) is the US board source.
- **US de minimis:** the $800 duty exemption is suspended for all countries, so AliExpress optics and panels pay duty and possibly an entry fee.
- **Japan small parcels:** a customs value of ¥10,000 or less is exempt from duty and consumption tax. Above it, add 10% tax and a carrier fee (DHL: ¥2,200). The rule changes on 2028-04-01, not before.
- **Japan radio law (技適):** ESP32-C3-MINI-1 has certificate 201-210888 and the XIAO ESP32C3 has 204-B00704 (seller's page). The QT Py ESP32-C3 and generic SuperMini boards have none: do not use them in Japan. A 180-day experimental notification exists for uncertified Wi-Fi and Bluetooth devices.
- **Japan battery law (PSE):** applies to cells of 400 Wh/L or more. A 500 mAh pouch cell works out at about 310 to 370 Wh/L from its outer size, under the line. This is a calculation, not a ruling.
- **LiPo shipping:** SparkFun does not ship cells abroad; cells travel by ground in the US. Buy cells in the country where they are used.

## What could not be confirmed

- **DigiKey US and Mouser.** Both refused automated reading, and a normal Chrome window driven by Playwright got a Cloudflare check on DigiKey and a refusal on Mouser. DigiKey's US prices and stock are the ones read on its Mexico storefront (digikey.com.mx, priced in US dollars). The three DigiKey pages that did load in Chrome matched those figures, which supports one shared inventory, but any US tariff surcharge at checkout is not included. Nothing was read at Mouser.
- **Read in Chrome on the second pass:** Edmund US and Japan, Thorlabs, Orient Display, Seeed, PiShop, K&J, MonotaRO, Sengoku, Amazon and AliExpress search pages.
- **Still unread:** Newark, Arrow, McMaster, Zoro, Taobao, Misumi and Marutsu.
- **Shipping fees:** only DigiKey US ($4.99), DigiKey Japan (¥2,000 under ¥6,000), Switch Science and OSH Park were read. Every other fee in the seller table is an estimate; fees appear only at checkout, and no cart was touched.
- **Japan:** magnets, a USB cable source, the battery's stock count, JLCPCB's price for these two boards, and delivery of the OLED panel from Orient's US store.
- **Pod-0 OLED:** Orient's page says SSD1315 and DigiKey says SSD1306 for the same part. Both are command-compatible in normal use, but read the datasheet before ordering.
- **Concept 5:** no HDMI to USB-C DisplayPort adapter for the glasses was confirmed in either country.
