5 · Shoot and send

Take a photo with the glasses, print it on e-paper

Aim

A head-locked target at the e-paper’s shape

Shoot

Only what’s inside the target, mapped into the camera image

Dither

Grey, contrast-stretched, Floyd–Steinberg to 1 bit

Send

Drawn on the maplet and saved to its roll

The permission trick: Bluetooth plus camera is refused. Declare the Internet module too and it becomes a Transparent Permission lens: one prompt, then it works.

The newest feature. CAMERA opens a target locked to your head at the maplet’s aspect ratio. SHOOT grabs a fresh camera frame and crops exactly what’s inside the target — its four corners are projected into the camera image, so a slight tilt still comes out straight. It’s turned grey, stretched and dithered to 1 bit, shown for five seconds, then lands in the Roll. SEND puts any selected frame on the maplet’s e-paper and saves new shots to its card. The gotcha: Spectacles refuse a lens that uses Bluetooth together with the camera. Declaring the Internet module as well — we never use it — makes it a Transparent Permission lens, and after one prompt it all works.
