Day one was the go/no-go: could the glasses talk to our devices without bonding? Yes — and heart rate was flowing to the glasses and the goggle by the end of it. Day two added the Remote, the Roll and the pin board, plus firmware 0.6.1 for goggl1 and 0.3.2 for the maplet. Day three was the camera, then fixes from wearing it. About 2,650 lines of TypeScript in the lens, not counting the SVG renderer we reused.
