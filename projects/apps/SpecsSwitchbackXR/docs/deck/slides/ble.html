The one rule

No bonding. No internet. Just Bluetooth.

~100 ms

round trip, lens to device

3

links at once, up within 4 s

0

pairing codes, clouds or SDKs

The go/no-go question on day one was: can a lens talk to our devices without bonding? Lens Studio’s Bluetooth module can’t bond and can’t raise the MTU, so writes go in 20-byte chunks. Our devices never needed encryption, so it just worked: about 100 ms median round trip, subscribed in 70 ms, and all three links up within 4 seconds of launch.
