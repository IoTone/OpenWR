The pin board draws a contour map in software and lays it on a tilted plate in front of you, with your track and pins. You can drop a pin, set auto-pins, and pick a destination. Each pin also stands in the room as a flag on its true bearing, labelled with distance and climb. Farther flags sit higher, like a horizon, and labels that would overlap step above each other. The flag on this slide is an example layout, not live data. Position is a demo walk for now; the phone feeds real GPS next.
