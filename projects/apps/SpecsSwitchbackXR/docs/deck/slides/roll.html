30 ms → 15 ms

The maplet keeps its photos on microSD as packed 1-bit frames. The lens lists them with roll-ls and pulls each one over the serial link. Hex was 8 to 11 seconds a frame; we added a base64 word to the firmware and asked the glasses for a 15 ms connection interval, which got it to 6 to 8 seconds. The MTU stays at 23 bytes, so the next win is on the maplet side.
