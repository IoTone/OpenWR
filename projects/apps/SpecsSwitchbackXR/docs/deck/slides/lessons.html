What we learned

Six Spectacles gotchas, so you skip them

What happenedWhat fixed it
Lens Bluetooth can’t bond; MTU stays 23No encryption on our devices; write in 20-byte chunks
WebView plus Bluetooth: deniedKeep the WebView package out of a Bluetooth lens
Camera plus Bluetooth: deniedDeclare the Internet module: one prompt, then allowed
Images flickered on the panelsZ-fighting with the frame’s glass; sit 3–4 mm in front

Log says “InternalError: interrupted”That’s a hung script being killed, not a restart
Six things that cost us time, so you don’t have to lose it. The permission ones are the big ones: a Bluetooth lens can’t include WebView, and it can’t use the camera unless it also declares the Internet module, which turns it into a Transparent Permission lens. The display ones are about the additive glass and the UI kit’s container frames. And if your Lens Studio log says InternalError interrupted, look for an infinite loop — ours froze the lens on launch.
