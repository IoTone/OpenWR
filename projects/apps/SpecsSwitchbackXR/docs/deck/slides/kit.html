The kit

Five things from around the house

Spectacles ’24

The lookout. Runs the lens and talks to everything else over Bluetooth.

Heart-rate strap

Scosche Rhythm 24 armband, standard heart-rate service. No SDK.

goggl1

A ski goggle with a 128×64 transparent OLED on an ESP32‑C3 Beetle.

maplet

XIAO ESP32‑S3 Sense: camera, microSD and a 2.13″ e-paper panel.

A phone

Switchback for Android: basecamp for GPS, maps and plans.

Nothing here was bought for this project. The glasses are Spectacles ’24. The strap is a Scosche Rhythm 24 — it speaks the standard Bluetooth heart-rate service, so we never needed Scosche’s SDK. goggl1 is a ski goggle with a DFRobot transparent OLED in front of one eye. The maplet is a Seeed XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense with its camera, a microSD and a small e-paper panel. Both little boards run Forth.
