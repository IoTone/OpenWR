On the glasses, black is clear glass, so each card is just the goggle’s lit pixels floating in the room.

goggl1 is a one-eye heads-up display. Its screens are rendered as cards at their real 128 by 64 pixels, so what you see in the glasses is exactly what the goggle will show. Pinching a card types a Forth word over Bluetooth and the goggle changes screen. Because the Spectacles display is additive, the black background disappears and only the lit pixels float.
