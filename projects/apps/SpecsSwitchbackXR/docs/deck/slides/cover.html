SwitchbackXR

The Switchback trail app on Spectacles, built in 3 days with gear from around the house

Spectacles ’24 · heart-rate strap · goggle HUD · e-paper camera

Hi, I’m [name] from IoTone. SwitchbackXR is our Switchback trail app (Android) brought onto Spectacles ’24. Over three days we tied the glasses to things that were already lying around: a heart-rate armband, a ski goggle with a transparent OLED, and a little e-paper camera we call the maplet. Everything talks Bluetooth; nothing touches the internet.
